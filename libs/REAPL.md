Reapl LLM

Your models. Your keys. Your conversation.

Reapl LLM is a BYOK — Bring Your Own Key AI platform built around the idea of giving users more control over how they use different AI models.

Reapl was not built around the idea that one model should be at the center of everything.

Instead, Reapl is designed as a place where users can bring their own providers and API keys, then use those models through one simple experience.

---

The Idea

AI is moving incredibly fast.

New models appear, providers change, pricing changes, capabilities change, and every user has different needs.

Reapl started with a simple question:

«Why should using multiple models always depend on a single platform?»

That question became the foundation of Reapl's BYOK approach.

Users bring their own API keys.

They are not forced to depend on a single model.

Reapl does not need to become the middleman for everything.

It simply becomes the place where everything comes together.

---

BYOK

Bring Your Own Key.

Reapl is designed around the idea that users should have greater control over their own model access.

Users can connect the providers they already use and choose models based on what they actually need.

Reapl is not about forcing everyone to use one model.

It is about creating a workspace for multiple models.

---

Multi-LLM

One conversation does not always need the same model.

Some models are better for reasoning.

Some are faster for simple questions.

Some are stronger at coding.

Some handle long contexts better.

Others may simply fit a particular workflow better.

Reapl brings that flexibility into one experience:

choose the model that fits the task.

---

A Different Kind of AI App

Reapl was not designed to become another dashboard filled with hundreds of features.

The focus is simpler:

conversation.

Model.

Context.

Input.

Output.

And an experience that does not make users feel like they are operating an unnecessarily complicated system.

---

The Reapl Philosophy

Reapl is built around a few principles.

User-Owned Access

Users bring their own API keys and control their own provider access.

Model Freedom

Reapl does not lock the experience around a single provider or model.

Simple Interface

Complexity should stay behind the interface.

The user experience should remain simple.

Local-First Thinking

Things that can remain on the user's side should not need to be unnecessarily moved to a server.

Open Ecosystem

The AI ecosystem will continue to change.

Reapl is designed with that reality in mind.

---

Reapl LLM

Reapl is not a single model.

It is not a new foundation model either.

Reapl is an experience layer built around the different LLMs its users choose to use.

One place to:

- choose a model
- connect a provider
- bring an API key
- create conversations
- switch between models
- maintain context
- and use AI according to your own workflow

Without making every provider feel like an entirely separate experience.

---

Built Around BYOK

With BYOK, users maintain a direct relationship with the providers they choose.

Reapl does not need to own every model.

It does not need to build its own foundation model.

It does not need to decide which model everyone should use.

Instead, Reapl focuses on building the experience around them.

«The models belong to the ecosystem.
The choice belongs to the user.»

---

More Than a Chat UI

Reapl started as an interface experiment and gradually became a project exploring how a multi-LLM experience could work.

Each iteration changes something about how conversations, models, attachments, outputs, and additional information are presented.

The goal is not to add as many features as possible.

The goal is to find an interface that feels natural when working with multiple models.

The Project

Reapl is part of the INCE ecosystem.

Reapl continues to change.

Previous versions may look completely different from future versions.

And that is part of the process.

Reapl is an evolving experiment.
